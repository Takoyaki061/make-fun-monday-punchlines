One joke about Monday mornings, entered in the
[Monday Morning Punchlines](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-monday-morning-punchlines-709645dc)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the joke to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> Monday morning, my toaster filed for emotional damages, the lamp
> testified, and I was sentenced to a second Monday as an adult.

The joke is 127 characters — under the 200-character limit. Ten
candidates were brainstormed (five with DeepSeek as second brain, five
authored) and critiqued against the judge's criteria; see
[CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the full list and the pick rationale.
