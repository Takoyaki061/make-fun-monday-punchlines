# Monday Morning Punchlines — candidates

Judge criteria, most important first: (1) really about Monday mornings;
(2) a real laugh, a punchline that lands, not a complaint; (3) surprise;
(4) economy of words; (5) freshness, no tired Monday cliches.

## Critique (in my own words)

- B1 — STRONGEST so far. Absurdist courtroom escalation is genuinely surprising,
  every word works, and the "second Monday as an adult" kicker is a real
  punchline, not a complaint. Fresh: no alarm, no coffee, no bed.
- B2 — strong. Dark twist, HR rescheduling a funeral is unexpected and
  Monday-specific (only Monday would do this). Slightly long setup.
- B3 — clever and meta, but it is a joke about writing a Monday joke rather
  than about Monday morning itself — risks criterion 1 against a truly
  Monday-centred rival.
- B4 — solid office joke, "stand was a metaphor and up was a threat" is a good
  turn, but the forty-minute stand-up is a mild complaint at its core.
- B5 — fresh treaty image, but the punchline is a shrug; more resignation
  than laugh.
- M1 — bed imagery is the tired cliche in another coat; fails freshness.
- M2 — decent roommate twist, but "charging rent" lands softly.
- M3 — meta and economical, but the laugh is small; the punchline is the
  premise itself.
- M4 — "put me on hold" is a weak kicker; mostly setup.
- M5 — fresh angle (week as software), but reads as an observation, not a
  joke with a landing punchline.
